## 2. Source-confirmed definitions and scope
- **CODE F / CODE FOXTROT:** Emergency code for flooding within One Bangkok.
- **Flood:** Flooding or flash flood arising from heavy continuous rain, tropical storms, monsoon troughs or dam failure.
- **Scope:** One Bangkok and surrounding areas that may affect the development, to protect customers, tenants and staff.
- **SOC:** Security Operation Centre; CCTV monitoring and security operations 24 hours.
- **FMC:** Facility Management Centre; building utilities, electrical and pumping systems.
- **TMT:** Traffic Management Team.
- **DCC:** District Command Centre.
- **BMO:** Building Management Operation.
- **EOT:** Emergency Operation Team.
- **IMT:** Incident Management Team; CAT 1/CAT 2 crisis management.
- **Stop Log:** Source states 12 ramp positions and five perimeter sectors: Wireless Road, Rama IV Road, Soi Pluk Chit, EXAT side and Embassy Road side.

## 3. Pre-Flooding Preparedness (source-confirmed)
1. **SOC:** Monitor weather and government information; notify DCC.
2. **TMT:** Arrange security patrols around the project.
3. **Engineering:** Check Retention Tank water gate; maintain stored water level at **no more than 15% of capacity** (as stated in source).
4. **Engineering:** Check readiness of basement pumps at **B3, B4 and B5**.
5. **EOT and TMT:** Drill Flood Wall / Stop Log installation.

## 4. During Flooding (source-confirmed)
### 4.1 Level 1 — Pre-Flooding / Watch
- TMT observes external flooding and informs SOC.
- SOC Operator opens a case in **Mozart**, notifies **DCC Manager**, **Contact Centre** and **EOT** for site inspection, and monitors CCTV.
- If flooding on **Wireless Road reaches at least 0.1 m and is rising**, **DCC Manager informs IMT** concerning announcement of **CODE F** and preparation for District Flooding. The precise internal IMT approval sequence is not established by this Markdown source.

### 4.2 Level 2 — District Flooding / CODE F
- **DCC Manager** announces CODE F over the **Incident Channel** and activates the **DCC Alignment** room.
- **Senior Technician** cuts electrical power in affected areas and operates drainage pumps at full capacity, per source description. This reference does not authorize unqualified personnel to work on electricity.
- **Stop Log stage 1:** Ramp and ground-floor Louver; group 1 points **1–6**, group 2 points **7–11**, per the supplied Markdown.
- **Stop Log stage 2:** At flood level **+1.35 MSL or higher**, install perimeter Stop Log to at least **+3.02 MSL**; source states group 1 points 1–2 and groups 3–5, without enough mapping detail to reconstruct the full site installation sequence.
- **TMT and BMO:** Manage traffic, signage, routes and parking.
- **As flood levels steadily decrease:** EOT informs SOC and DCC to confirm decreasing levels and coordinate removal of Stop Log and cancellation of CODE F. The Markdown does **not** clearly specify the final cancellation approval/sign-off chain.

## 5. Post-Flooding / Recovery (source-confirmed)
DCC coordinates recovery assessment in five areas:
1. Buildings and main work locations.
2. Information technology and critical information.
3. Important materials and equipment.
4. Key personnel.
5. Partners, critical service providers and stakeholders.

Related documents listed in source: `OB-SOP-LW-0001` incident/accident investigation and reporting; `OB-FR-LW-0101` incident/accident report form; Flood Risk Study; One Bangkok EIA.
