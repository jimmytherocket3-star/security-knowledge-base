## 1. Definition / Scope
**Code D = Deceased Person / พบผู้เสียชีวิต**.

This reference covers the One Bangkok standard operating procedure for management when a deceased person is found within the project area.

---

## 2. Source-Supported Purpose
The approved source describes an SOP intended to establish a systematic response for a deceased-person incident within One Bangkok and to align incident handling with safety and legal requirements.

The source summary states that the procedure covers the incident lifecycle from:
- Incident notification.
- Situation assessment.
- Coordination with external agencies, including police and forensic medical personnel.
- Defined roles/responsibilities of relevant internal functions, including Security, emergency-response operations, and control-center functions.
- Measures intended to support timely incident management and reduce impact on the project's image/reputation.
- A Swimlane Flowchart illustrating the overall operating process.
