# Building Management Legal Reference — Thailand

## 1. Source Boundary and Status
**Draft Legal Research Reference** based solely on the KB Owner-supplied file **กฎหมายที่เกี่ยวกับการบริหารอาคาร.md** (received 2026-10-11). This document records claims in that source; it does **not** independently verify statutory text, court decisions, effective versions, or applicability to any particular One Bangkok building. It is not legal advice, an Approved legal interpretation, or an operational SOP. Do not convert its claims into staff powers or mandatory site instructions.

The supplied file contains bracketed citation numbers [1]–[33] without a complete, traceable bibliography/URLs. Those markers do not constitute independently verified citations.

## 2. Building Control Act
The supplied source identifies the **Building Control Act B.E. 2522 (1979), as amended**, as governing construction, alteration, demolition, use, and changes of building use.

Source-described provisions requiring official verification:
- **Section 32 bis:** inspection by qualified registered engineering/architectural inspectors for specified building types; building condition, structure, electrical, fire protection, ventilation/water/wastewater and mechanical systems; reporting to local officials for building inspection certification.
- **Sections 46 and 46 bis:** local official measures concerning unsafe or dangerous buildings, including correction and, in serious cases, demolition.
- **Section 65 bis:** source states penalties for failure to comply with Section 32 bis of imprisonment up to three months or fine up to THB 60,000 or both, plus daily fine up to THB 10,000. **Do not treat these amounts as verified current law.**

The source mentions Supreme Court decisions **4733/2541, 2566/2543, 2860/2537** in this context. Exact holdings and applicability remain unverified.

## 3. High-Rise / Extra-Large Building Safety
The source identifies **Ministerial Regulation No. 33 (B.E. 2535)**, describing high-rise buildings as at least 23 m and extra-large buildings as at least 10,000 sq.m. total area. It attributes automatic sprinkler coverage to Clause 20 and fire-resistant stair enclosure requirements to Clause 8 bis. **These provision-level claims require checking against official texts and amendments.**

## 4. Condominium Management
The source identifies **Condominium Act B.E. 2522 (1979), as amended**, and describes:
- Section 15: common property.
- Section 18: shared common expenses.
- Section 33: juristic person's management and maintenance of common property.
- Sections 35–36: condominium manager's powers/duties.
- Sections 37–38: condominium committee oversight.

The source also lists Supreme Court decisions **2369/2557, 3220/2553, 8512/2553, 32/2542, 1143/2541, 13225/2553** regarding common/private property and common expenses. These are **source-described, not independently verified**; no generalized legal conclusion should be drawn without checking originals.

## 5. Hotel Management
The source identifies **Hotel Act B.E. 2547 (2004), Section 34**, describing operator/manager responsibilities for cleanliness, building safety, fire prevention, environmental quality, and assistance to guests during fire/flood. The statutory text and its applicability to specific facilities remain unverified.

## 6. Other Referenced Rules
The supplied source additionally identifies:
- Ministerial regulation on earthquake-resistant structural design, B.E. 2564.
- Ministerial regulation on building structural design and materials, B.E. 2566.
- Ministerial regulation requiring liability insurance for certain buildings, B.E. 2564.
- Workplace fire prevention and suppression ministerial regulation, B.E. 2555, linked by the source to the Occupational Safety, Health and Environment Act B.E. 2554.
- Engineers Act B.E. 2542 and Architects Act B.E. 2543.
- Local ordinances under the Building Control Act; applicable locality-specific rules need confirmation.
- Source notes the 2548 private-building-inspector regulations and a need to check the original Royal Gazette.
- Source explicitly flags Ministerial Regulation No. 47 (B.E. 2537) as not found in its checked collection and needing verification from the original Gazette.

These are research pointers, not verified compliance instructions.
