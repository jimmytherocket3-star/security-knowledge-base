# พระราชบัญญัติธุรกิจรักษาความปลอดภัย พ.ศ. 2558 — Legal Research Reference

## 1. Source Boundary
This **Draft** records a KB Owner-supplied summary titled `# พ.ร.บ ธุรกิจรักษาความปลอดภัย2558.md` (received 2026-10-11). The original text of the Act, its amendments, regulations, case judgments, and currently applicable penalties have **not** been independently checked. Statements below are **source-described claims**, not independently verified legal conclusions, approved staff powers, or operational SOP.

## 2. Principles and Coverage (Source-Described)
- **Section 3:** defines security business as paid provision of licensed security personnel protecting life, body, or property; mentions government-service exclusions.
- **Section 16:** source says the operator must be a licensed company, not an individual or partnership.
- **Section 4:** source describes central and provincial registrars, including Bangkok.

## 3. Security Company Licensing and Contracts
- **Section 17:** source lists Thai-majority shareholding and board membership, and disqualification conditions.
- **Sections 18–19:** source describes 60-day registrar consideration and four-year license duration.
- **Sections 20–21:** source describes licensed company naming requirements.
- **Sections 23, 25:** source states a written service contract must describe scope, term, fees, responsibilities, penalties/damages and termination; source claims a noncompliant contract is void. **Verify the exact statutory consequence and clauses before relying on this.**

## 4. Security Operator Duties
- **Section 26:** source describes standards for supervision, daily incident logs, coordination centers, communications, and refresher training.
- **Section 27:** source describes providing licensed security personnel to clients.
- **Sections 28–29:** source describes police cooperation on logs and immediate notification of crime/intelligence.
- **Section 30:** source describes employee list reporting within 30 days and changes within 15 days. **Verify current timelines.**

## 5. Licensed Security Personnel
- **Section 34:** source lists Thai nationality, age 18+, compulsory education, approved training, and disqualifying conditions.
- **Sections 39–40:** source describes uniforms, identification and restrictions on resembling official uniforms.
- **Section 41:** source describes (1) assisting administrative/police officers in arrests under criminal procedure, (2) protection of life/body/property, incident containment and order in contracted area, (3) prompt notification of crime or serious incidents and preservation of scene pending arrival of authorities.

**Authority guardrail:** Section 41 summaries must **not** be treated as blanket powers to arrest, search, seize, detain, or use force. Applicable criminal procedure, necessity/proportionality and approved site SOPs require separate verification. Do not invent arrest or evidence handling workflows.

## 6. Penalties (Unverified)
- **Section 55:** source states unlicensed security business: up to one year imprisonment / THB 20,000 fine / both.
- **Section 63:** source states acting as security personnel without a license: up to three months imprisonment / THB 5,000 fine / both.
- **Sections 59, 60, 64, 68, 69:** source mentions other criminal penalties and possible director/manager responsibility.
- **Section 58 and B.E. 2565 Act on Imposition of Non-Criminal (Pinai) Fines:** source claims some offenses converted to noncriminal fines, giving THB 100,000 as an example.

**Do not assert any penalty, offense classification, or Pinai conversion as currently applicable without checking official legislation and amendments.**

## 7. Related Legal Context in Supplied Source
- Labor relationship between security company and security personnel; **the source's broad claim about employment status must be verified against actual arrangements and applicable labor law**.
- Proportional use of force, citing Supreme Court cases **1080/2507, 3045/2526, 1371/2540**.
- Customer property / parking area security, citing **6099/2560**.
- Building Control Act cross-reference and general building management responsibilities.
- **Section 74:** historical transitional arrangements for existing security personnel; do not apply these historical deadlines to current staff without verification.
- Fee regulation B.E. 2560; example license fee THB 5,000 for companies with fewer than 100 staff, unverified.
- Source notes that cases cited are generally about legal principles rather than direct judicial interpretation of the 2558 Act.
