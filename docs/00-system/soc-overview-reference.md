# SOC Overview Reference — One Bangkok

## Source Scope

## Overview

The document summarizes the **Security Operation Center (SOC)** within **One Bangkok**, a large mixed-use development in central Bangkok.

The source states that the SOC-related information covers:

- management structure;
- areas of responsibility divided into zones;
- intelligent surveillance technology;
- incident management through the **MOZART** platform;
- a high-performance CCTV network;
- standard operating procedures, including readiness checks before starting work;
- virtual patrol activities;
- emergency response using specific communication codes;
- threat-level classification; and
- coordination among internal units within the central control center.

The described security coverage includes office, retail, and residential areas on a 24-hour basis.

## Source-Stated Objective

The source identifies the principal objective as establishing a foundation that enables personnel to respond to incidents quickly and efficiently according to international standards.
