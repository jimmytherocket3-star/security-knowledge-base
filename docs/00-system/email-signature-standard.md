# Email Signature Standard — Senses Property Management Company Limited

## Purpose
Define the mandatory company signature image placement for email drafts prepared for the KB Owner.

## Approved Signature Image
Use the company signature image supplied and approved by the KB Owner on 2026-09-28.

The approved artwork contains:
- SENSES PROPERTY MANAGEMENT logo
- Senses Property Management Company Limited
- 90 CW Tower, Tower A, Unit A1801, 18th Floor, Ratchadaphisek Road, Huai Khwang, Huai Khwang, Bangkok 10310, Thailand
- +66 2643 7595
- www.senses.co.th

## Mandatory Placement Rule
For every email prepared for the KB Owner, place the actual approved signature image inline immediately after the KB Owner's contact information.

This applies to all email types, including Guard Tour reports, Contact Center emails, Incident / operational reports, and other operational email drafts.

## Rendering Rule
- Use the actual approved image.
- Do not replace the image with text such as `[โลโก้บริษัท]`.
- Do not treat a normal file attachment as a substitute for the inline signature image.
- The signature image must appear after the KB Owner's contact information in the email body.
- Recipient / CC information remains specific to each email and must not be inferred when not established.

## Source
Direct KB Owner instruction and approved signature artwork supplied on 2026-09-28.
