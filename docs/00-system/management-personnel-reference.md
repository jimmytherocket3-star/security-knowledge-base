1. Use the table only as a mapping between the source-provided personnel labels and One00–One05 codes.
2. Do not infer job title, department, reporting line, authority level, telephone number, or emergency responsibility where the approved source does not state it.
3. Do not automatically treat any person in this directory as Incident Commander, DCC authority, IMT authority, Code Sierra authority, Code 3 authority, All Clear Authority, or other emergency decision-maker.
4. Emergency authority and responsibilities must come from the relevant approved Emergency Procedure / SOP, not from this personnel reference.
5. Preserve source-provided spelling and nicknames unless the KB Owner approves a correction.
6. Do not identify or infer additional facts about the people from their photographs.
7. Personnel changes, code changes, corrections, or additional management mappings require KB Owner approval before KB modification.
